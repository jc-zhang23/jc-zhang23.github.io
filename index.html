Hi! I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University, expecting to graduate in June 2027. I am conducting research with Prof. Meng Jiang in the DM2 Lab at the University of Notre Dame.

My research focuses on trustworthy AI, particularly LLM interpretability and answerability. I study how experimental choices affect what we conclude about language models: how to compare interventions on their internal representations, and how to predict whether a passage contains enough information to answer a question.

I also have experience in multimodal learning, human sensing, and vehicle trajectory prediction.

Strength Controls Change How Interventions Compare Across Sparse Autoencoder Codes

Jinchen Zhang*, Zheyuan Liu*, Yihan Zhu, Meng Jiang

arXiv preprint, 2026 · Under review at ACL Rolling Review (ARR)

How should we hold intervention strength constant when studying an LLM? We compare norm matching and output calibration across Gemma and Llama settings, showing that the choice of control changes agreement with a reference intervention.

Training Negatives Still Matter After Calibrating Answerability Readouts

Jinchen Zhang, Zheyuan Liu, Yihan Zhu, Meng Jiang

arXiv preprint, 2026 · Under review at ARR

Can calibration compensate for removing unanswerable passages during training? On SQuAD 2.0 and RepLiQA, we find that training negatives affect prediction, ranking, and answer retention even after calibration, with results depending on the estimator and model layer.
